## Part 2

12-16句

### People

#### Celebrity

Describe a popular/well-known person in your country. 

You should say:

-  Who this person is
  - identity
    - 有名气的 ：well-received / go down very well
  - personal info
    - born in
    - university
    - competition / champion / medals
  - physical appearance
    - 胖 ：over-weight / big-boned
    - 瘦 ：thin / slim
    - 性格 ：extroverted 外向的， enthusiastic 热情的，versatile 多才多艺的，compassionate 有同情心的， benevolent 乐善好施的
- **What he/she has done**
  - achievement / competition
  - how successful / awards
  - contribution -> donation, education
  - positive influence
- Why he/she is popular
  - reasons / evidence
  - social media post

- And explain how you feel about this person
  - Why you like/admire this person

#### Average person

Describe an interesting neighbor. 

开头点题：Speaking of an interesting and creative/talented girl who lives next door ,the one that pops up in my head is my friend, Sally.

You should say:

- Who he/she is
- How you knew him/her 
  - I firstly knew her was when I ...
- What you do together
- And explain why he/she is interesting
  - Moving onto how I feel about her/ Why I believe she is interesting and creative....

### place

- Where it is
- What it is famous for 
- How you knew this place
- Why you think it is very interesting

### Event

#### type

1. family event
   - birthday
   - anniversary
   - birthday part/banque
2. social event
   - school reunion
3. school event
   - freshman welcome party
   - new year's party
   - competition
   - job fair

#### common

Describe a time when you organized a happy event successfully. You should say:

- What the event was
  - why
- How you prepared for it
- Who helped you to organize it
- And explain why you think it was a successful event

Describe a special day out that cost you little money/didn't cost you much. You should say:

- When the day was
  - why
- Where you went
  - why
- How much you spent
  - what you did there
- And explain how you feel about the day
  - how your friends feel

### Object

Describe something you do to keep fit and healthy. You should say:

- What it is
  - Why you need it
- When you do it
  - How often
- Who you do it with
- And explain why you think this method is 

Describe a tradtional product in your country. You should say:

- What it is
- When you tried this product for the first time
- What it is made of + How it's made
- And explain how you feel about it
